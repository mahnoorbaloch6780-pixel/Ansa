## Brand & Style
The design system manifests an enchanted, sunlit garden library where fairy tales and literary fantasies live. It blends ethereal dreaminess with tactile warmth—reminiscent of gilded edges, aged deckle paper, gentle petal-toned watercolor washes, and warm midday sun streaming through tall arched windows.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **High-End Editorial Literary Romanticism** with **Delicate Tactile Layering**:
- Atmosphere is warm, inviting, gentle, and imaginative without becoming juvenile.
- Surfaces simulate translucent vellum, pressed flower petals, and soft-touch bookcloth.
- Details incorporate subtle celestial, starlight, and botanical motifs paired with thin metallic-gold embellishments.
- Motion and interactions feel like gentle rustling pages, shimmering starbursts, and floating dandelion seeds.

## Colors
The palette balances luminous parchment foundations with ink-rich dusky violets and shimmering, sun-drenched gold highlights. Pastel washes organize categories and book realms with airy distinction.

### Foundations & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#FFFBF5`)**: Warm cream vellum ground for main page canvas.
- **Ivory Parchment (`#FDF9F2`)**: Slightly richer paper tint for elevated modules, drawers, and nested containers.
- **Translucent Pearlescent Surface (`rgba(255, 253, 249, 0.78)`)**: Glass-frosted parchment for overlaid panels and floating navigation.

### Inks & Hierarchy
- **Midnight Plum (`#4A3B4F`)**: Deepest ink for headings, primary content, and high-emphasis boundaries. Provides high contrast while feeling softer than stark black.
- **Muted Thistle Plum (`#6C5B72`)**: Mid-tier ink for metadata, excerpts, captions, and unfocused states.
- **Subtle Faded Ink (`#9E8F9F`)**: Secondary metadata, inactive states, and decorative dividers.

### Gilded Highlights & Magic
- **Burnished Antique Gold (`#D4A359`)**: Primary accent used for primary CTAs, active states, gilded filigree lines, and star sparkle highlights.
- **Sunlit Gold Sheen (`#E5C07B`)**: Subtle glow states, luminous borders, and hover halo layers.

### Story Realm Pastels
- **Blush Rose (`#F7D6E0`)**: Romance, fairytale retellings, wishlists, and curated recommendations.
- **Lavender Mist (`#E4D9F5`)**: High fantasy, celestial fiction, and audiobooks.
- **Enchanted Mint (`#D8F0E3`)**: Mythological fiction, magical realism, and eco/cottagecore tags.
- **Gossamer Azure (`#DCEBFA`)**: Epic sagas, winter folklore, and reading tracker states.
- **Sweet Peach (`#FDE5D4`)**: Staff favorites, collector editions, and promotional badges.

## Typography
The typographic system pairs the stately, romantic flair of **Playfair Display** with the clear, modern accessibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Use Playfair Display. Large headings should feature balanced optical kerning. Italic styles can be selected sparingly for literary pull quotes, book chapter tags, or highlighted adjectives to inject whimsical flourish.
- **Body & Narratives:** Plus Jakarta Sans ensures long synopses, reviews, and transaction details remain effortless to read against warm parchment. Set line heights generously (1.5–1.6x) to mimic the spacing of high-grade book typesets.
- **All-Caps Tags & Labels:** Use tracking of `+0.06em` to `+0.08em` on small labels to preserve clean legibility and convey the feel of embossed book spine stamping.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout uses a fluid 12-column grid on desktop and tablet, resolving down to 4 columns on mobile. It relies on airy breathing room reminiscent of broad book margins.

### Breakpoints & Rules
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column grid, max container width `1360px`, `margin-lg` (64px) canvas padding, `gutter-lg` (32px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid, `margin` (32px), `gutter` (24px).
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** 4-column grid, `margin-sm` (16px), `gutter-sm` (16px).

Sections maintain ample vertical rhythm with minimum `space-xl` separation to cultivate a serene, unhurried browsing journey.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through **luminous translucency and warm ambient feathering** rather than sharp dropped shadows.

### Atmospheric Shadow Styles
- **Vellum Rest (Cards & Tiles):** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(74, 59, 79, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.08)`
- **Hover & Magic State:** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(74, 59, 79, 0.12), 0 0 18px 2px rgba(229, 192, 123, 0.35)`
- **Modal & Popover Tiers:** `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(74, 59, 79, 0.18), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.35)`

### Gilded Borders & Translucency
Components utilize a subtle inner border: `1px solid rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.22)`. Dynamic elements feature frosted glass overlays (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) combined with warm ivory color stops (`rgba(253, 249, 242, 0.82)`).

## Shapes
Shapes evoke smooth hand-cut paper, rounded leather corner protectors, and organic botanical curves. 

- **Level 2 Standard:** Standard interactive elements (buttons, inputs) employ `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px) corners.
- **Large Surfacing (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`):** Book cards and elevated vellum panels feature `1rem` (16px) or `1.5rem` (24px) corners.
- **Pill Shapes:** Category badges, ribbons, and floating chips leverage full pill curvature (`9999px`) to contrast with structural rectangular book jackets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Golden Spark"):** Background gradient of `#D4A359` to `#E5C07B`, text color `#FFFFFF` (with `#4A3B4F` text shadow or solid dark ink fill on smaller screens). 12px rounded radius. Hover initiates a golden halo pulse (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(229, 192, 123, 0.6)`) and a delicate +1px upward lift.
- **Secondary ("Parchment & Filigree"):** Translucent ivory base, `1px solid #D4A359` outline, `#4A3B4F` typography. Hover switches background to a tinted pastel hue (`#F7D6E0` or `#E4D9F5` at 20% opacity).
- **Icon / Whimsical Buttons:** Circular or pill-shaped buttons with fine hairline gold rims for wishlists (sparkle icon, bookmark icon).

### Storybook Cards
- Ivory parchment (`#FDF9F2`) surface, `24px` radius, subtle gold micro-border (`1px solid rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.2)`).
- Book cover imagery sits within an inset framed container with a subtle inner spine gradient simulating physical book volume.
- Hover action triggers a floating parchment effect with an ambient warm gold-violet drop shadow.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- Fully rounded pills (`9999px`) tinted in pastel washes:
  - Romantasy: `#F7D6E0` background with `#4A3B4F` text.
  - Lore & Spells: `#E4D9F5` background with `#4A3B4F` text.
  - Cottagecore & Myths: `#D8F0E3` background with `#4A3B4F` text.
- Optional micro star glyph (`✦`) prefixes preceding the tag text.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Ivory paper fill (`#FFFFFF` with 70% opacity), deep violet text (`#4A3B4F`), and placeholders in muted plum (`#6C5B72`).
- Resting border: `1px solid rgba(108, 91, 114, 0.25)`.
- Active focus state: `1px solid #D4A359` accompanied by a soft gilded glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(229, 192, 123, 0.25)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Rounded checkboxes (4px radius) and circular radio rings rimmed in `#6C5B72`.
- Checked state fills with `#D4A359`, displaying a crisp ivory checkmark or a gilded celestial dot.

### Special Accent Modules
- **Deckle Edge Dividers:** Horizontal separation lines accented by a centered starburst glyph (`✧`) in `#D4A359`.
- **Collector's Ribbon Badges:** Angled or top-hanging ribbon banner elements in `#D4A359` with scalloped or swallowtail bottom cuts for "Signed Edition", "Exclusive Binding", or "Staff Favorite".
